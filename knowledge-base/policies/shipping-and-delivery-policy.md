# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Welcome to StyleSense! We are committed to delivering your fashion favorites swiftly and securely across India. Please read our shipping and delivery policy carefully to understand how we process and deliver your orders.

## Shipping Options and Delivery Timeframes

StyleSense offers standard and express shipping options to suit your needs.

- **Standard Shipping**  
  - Cost: ₹50 per order  
  - Delivery Time: 5 to 7 business days  
  - Coverage: All major cities and towns across India  
  - Tracking: Provided via SMS and email within 24 hours of dispatch  

- **Express Shipping**  
  - Cost: ₹150 per order  
  - Delivery Time: 2 to 3 business days  
  - Coverage: Metro cities including Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune  
  - Tracking: Real-time tracking via our website and app  

*Please note:*  
- Orders placed before 3 PM IST on a business day are processed the same day; orders placed after 3 PM are processed the next business day.  
- Business days exclude Sundays and national holidays.  
- Some remote areas may require additional delivery days. We will inform you during checkout if additional time is expected.

## Order Processing and Shipping Conditions

- Minimum order value for delivery is ₹299.  
- We currently ship only within India. International shipping is not available.  
- All orders are carefully packed in eco-friendly materials to ensure product safety during transit.  
- For high-value orders over ₹10,000, we offer optional insurance at no extra cost. This covers loss or damage during shipping.  
- Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a confirmation email and SMS containing tracking details.  

**Address Accuracy:**  
Please provide complete and accurate delivery details, including postal codes and phone numbers. StyleSense is not responsible for delays or failed deliveries due to incorrect or incomplete addresses.

**Order Modifications:**  
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement by contacting our customer care at 1800-123-4567. After this window, we proceed with packing and shipment.
