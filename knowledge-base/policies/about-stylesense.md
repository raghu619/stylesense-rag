# About StyleSense, Our Story and What We Stand For

Welcome to **StyleSense**, your ultimate destination for contemporary Indian fashion that blends tradition with trend. Launched in 2018, StyleSense was created to redefine how modern India shops for apparel—bringing curated styles, high quality, and affordability together under one digital roof.

## Our Story

StyleSense began as a small start-up in Mumbai with a simple but ambitious vision: to make authentic, stylish Indian wear accessible to everyone, no matter where they live. Founded by fashion enthusiast Riya Kapoor and tech entrepreneur Arjun Mehta, StyleSense started as a curated collection of traditional sarees, kurtas, and lehengas sourced from artisan communities across Rajasthan, Gujarat, and West Bengal.

Within the first year of operation, we partnered with over 50 local craftspeople and small-scale mills, empowering their livelihoods while providing customers with handcrafted, eco-friendly products. Our online platform went live in June 2018, and by the end of our first year, we had served more than 10,000 happy customers in 12 Indian states.

Today, StyleSense features over 3,000 products, ranging from casual everyday wear to festive collections designed exclusively for major Indian festivals such as Diwali, Navratri, and Eid. We have grown to serve over 500,000 customers nationwide and shipping now covers all 28 states plus 8 union territories, with delivery times averaging 3-5 business days.

## What We Stand For

At StyleSense, we stand on three core pillars: **Authenticity, Sustainability, and Customer-Centricity**.

### Authenticity  
We believe in preserving the rich heritage of Indian textiles and craftsmanship. Every product on our site is either handcrafted or designed with close consultation from traditional artisans, ensuring genuine patterns, materials, and techniques. For example, our popular Bandhani collection comes directly from artisans in Kutch with natural tie-dye methods passed down for generations.

### Sustainability  
Fashion should be responsible. We prioritize eco-friendly fabrics such as organic cotton, khadi, and handwoven silks, reducing our environmental footprint. Our packaging is 100% recyclable, and we offset carbon emissions from delivery by planting trees in collaboration with local NGOs. Since 2020, we have reduced plastic use by 80% and recycled over 20 tons of fabric waste through upcycling initiatives.

### Customer-Centricity  
Shopping with StyleSense is designed to be easy, reliable, and transparent. We offer free delivery on orders over ₹999 and provide a 30-day return window with free return shipping anywhere in India. Our customer support team is available 7 days a week from 9 AM to 9 PM via chat, phone, and email. Customer satisfaction is our priority, demonstrated by a 4.8-star average rating from over 15,000 reviews.
