# Returns and Exchanges Policy

Welcome to StyleSense! We strive to ensure you love every item you order. However, if you are not fully satisfied with your purchase, our Returns and Exchanges Policy is designed to provide a smooth and hassle-free experience.

## Return Policy

At StyleSense, you may return most items within **15 calendar days** of receiving your order for a full refund or exchange. To be eligible for a return:

- The item must be unworn, unwashed, and in the original condition with all tags attached.
- The original packaging should be intact, including any accessories, manuals, or gifts.
- Sale items and gift cards are **non-returnable**.
- Personalized or custom-made items cannot be returned unless defective.

### Return Process

1. Notify us by emailing returns@stylesense.in or calling our customer care at **1800-123-4567** within 15 days of delivery.
2. Include your order number, item details, and reason for return.
3. Upon approval, we will provide you with a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and shipping instructions.
4. Ship the item back to us within **7 days** of receiving the RMA using a trackable courier service. We recommend using India Post, DTDC, or FedEx.
5. Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund will be processed within **7-10 business days** to your original payment method.

Please note that return shipping charges are the customer’s responsibility, except in cases where the item is defective or incorrect.

## Exchange Policy

If you would like to exchange an item for a different size, color, or style, please follow the same process as returns:

- Submit your exchange request within **15 days** of receiving your order.
- Exchanges are subject to stock availability.
- Once we receive the returned item and confirm it meets the conditions, we will ship the replacement item within **5 business days**.
- If the replacement item is of a different price, a refund or additional payment will be processed accordingly.
- Return shipping charges for exchanges are to be borne by the customer unless the error is on our part.

## Additional Information

- Items marked as "Final Sale" cannot be returned or exchanged.
- Defective or damaged products will be replaced or refunded after inspection. Please report any defects within **48 hours** of delivery.
- Refunds may take additional days to reflect depending on your bank or payment provider.
- For any assistance or questions about returns and exchanges, please reach out to our customer support team Monday through Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM IST.

Thank you for shopping with StyleSense. We appreciate your trust and aim to make your shopping experience delightful!
