# Sizing Chart and Fit Policy

Welcome to StyleSense, your trusted destination for contemporary Indian fashion. To ensure you find the perfect fit, we provide detailed sizing information and a transparent fit policy. Please read through the sizing chart and our fit guidelines carefully before placing your order.

## Sizing Chart

At StyleSense, we offer a diverse range of sizes to suit every body type, from traditional Indian wear to modern fusion styles. Below is our comprehensive sizing chart based on standard Indian measurements:

| Size | Bust (inches) | Waist (inches) | Hips (inches) | Length (inches) |
|-------|---------------|----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| XS    | 32 - 34       | 26 - 28        | 34 - 36       | 40-42           |
| S     | 34 - 36       | 28 - 30        | 36 - 38       | 42-44           |
| M     | 36 - 38       | 30 - 32        | 38 - 40       | 44-46           |
| L     | 38 - 40       | 32 - 34        | 40 - 42       | 46-48           |
| XL    | 40 - 42       | 34 - 36        | 42 - 44       | 48-50           |
| XXL   | 42 - 44       | 36 - 38        | 44 - 46       | 50-52           |

**Note:**  
- All measurements are taken in inches and are approximate.  
- Length refers to the garment length (from shoulder to hem for tops and kurtas).  
- For sarees, dupattas, and lehengas, length varies and is specified in individual product descriptions.

**How to Measure:**  
- Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal.  
- Waist: Measure around the natural waistline, just above the belly button.  
- Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips.  
- Length: Measured from shoulder to hemline for tops and kurtas.

If you're between sizes, we recommend choosing the larger size for comfort.

## Fit Policy

We want you to love your StyleSense purchase. To ensure your satisfaction, please review our fit policy carefully:

### Exchange and Return Window  
- Returns or exchanges are accepted within **10 days** of receiving your order.  
- Please initiate the return or exchange through your StyleSense account or contact our customer support at support@stylesense.in.

### Conditions for Returns or Exchanges  
- The garment must be in unused, unwashed, and original condition with all tags attached.  
- Items marked as **‘Final Sale’** or **‘Custom Tailored’** are not eligible for return or exchange.  
- Personalized or embroidered items cannot be returned unless faulty.

### Return Process  
1. Submit a return request within 10 days of delivery through your account dashboard.  
2. Pack the item securely in the original packaging.  
3. Our courier partner will collect the package from your address within 3 working days after approval.  
4. Once we receive and inspect the item, refunds will be processed within 7 business days through the original payment mode.

### Refunds  
- Refunds will exclude shipping charges unless the return is due to our error (wrong item, defective product).  
- Exchange-related shipping costs are borne by the customer unless the item was faulty.

### Custom Tailoring & Alterations  
- We offer custom tailoring services for selected products. Please allow **7-10 business days** for tailoring before shipment.  
- Custom-tailored items cannot be exchanged or returned. Please provide accurate measurements to avoid fit issues.
