# What to Wear to an Indian Wedding as a Guest

Attending an Indian wedding is a vibrant and joyous occasion that calls for stylish and culturally respectful attire. At StyleSense, we know choosing the right outfit can be daunting, so here’s practical, no-nonsense advice to nail your look without overspending or overthinking it.

## Men: Traditional Looks That Work

For men, Indian weddings are the perfect time to go beyond the usual suit. Opt for a **kurta pajama** in silk or fine cotton, paired with a **bandhgala jacket** or a **Nehru jacket** for a smarter edge. Colors like royal blue, maroon, beige, or emerald green are wedding staples — avoid plain black or white as these tend to be reserved for other occasions.

- **Footwear:** Go for traditional **mojari shoes** or leather loafers in tan or brown.
- **Accessories:** A silk stole or a brooch can add subtle sophistication, but keep jewelry minimal — a classy wristwatch works well.

## Women: Elegant & Comfortable Choices

Women should embrace vibrant colors and rich textures, but focus on comfort and ease given the usually long ceremonies.

- **Sarees:** Lightweight silk or chiffon sarees with delicate zari work (metallic thread embroidery) are ideal. Colors like magenta, royal purple, and mustard are popular. Pair with matching or gold-tone jewelry.
- **Lehengas:** For a slightly more formal look, a lehenga with floral or mirror work is a great pick. StyleSense offers ready-to-wear lehenga sets that combine modern cuts with traditional embellishments.
- **Anarkali suits:** These long, flowing dresses are comfortable and elegant, suitable for daytime events.
- **Footwear:** Closed-toe sandals or embellished flats work best since you’ll likely be on your feet a lot.
- **Accessories:** Statement earrings and bangles are classic; avoid overdoing it. A small clutch or potli bag completes the look.
- **Styling Tip:** Pick breathable fabrics like georgette or crepe to stay comfortable during outdoor or multi-day festivities.

## General Dos and Don’ts

- **Do** check the invitation for any dress code specifics (some weddings request all-white garments or specific colors).
- **Don’t** wear casual Western clothes like jeans or plain T-shirts—these are considered too informal.
- **Do** plan clothing in layers; Indian weddings often have different events (haldi, sangeet, reception) with varying formality.
- **Don’t** over-accessorize; elegant simplicity is often the best way to blend tradition with your personal style.
