# Fabric guide: linen, cotton, denim and blends

Welcome to StyleSense’s practical fabric guide. Choosing the right fabric can make all the difference in comfort, style, and durability. Here, we break down four popular fabrics—linen, cotton, denim, and blends—with actionable tips to help you select and style pieces confidently.

## Linen: Cool, Crisp, and Breathable

Linen is perfect for India’s hot climate. Made from flax fibers, linen garments—like kurtas, dresses, and trousers—offer maximum breathability and a lightweight feel.

- **Styling tip:** Linen naturally wrinkles, adding to its casual charm. Pair a white or beige linen kurta with tapered cotton chinos for a relaxed yet refined look.
- **Care advice:** Wash linen in cold water and air dry to maintain shape. Iron on medium heat if you want a polished look.
- **Best for:** Summer wear, casual outings, and office wear during warmer months.

Pro tip: Linen blends with cotton reduce wrinkling while retaining breathability—ideal for travel.

## Cotton: Versatile and Comfortable

Cotton is India’s most widely used fabric and suits all seasons depending on weave and weight. From soft cambric saris to thick cotton trousers, it’s breathable and easy to maintain.

- **Styling tip:** For everyday wear, try a printed cotton kurti with jeans or cotton palazzo pants. Choose lightweight cotton voile or cambric for festive wear; pair with embroidered juttis.
- **Care advice:** Machine wash in warm water and tumble dry low. Cotton shrinks slightly after the first wash, so consider sizing up for fitted garments.
- **Best for:** Everyday wear, casual office outfits, and semi-formal events.

Pro tip: Look for organic or handwoven cotton options to support sustainable fashion and get unique textures.

## Denim and Blends: Durable and Modern

Denim stands out as a rugged, stylish choice for casual and semi-casual wardrobes. StyleSense carries both classic indigo and lighter washed denims, including blends with elastane for stretch.

- **Styling tip:** Pair high-rise denim jeans with tucked-in cotton tees or linen shirts for an effortless look. For women, try denim jackets over cotton kurtis or casual dresses.
- **Care advice:** Wash denim inside out in cold water to preserve color; avoid frequent washing to extend garment life.
- **Blends:** Cotton-polyester blends combine the softness of cotton with the durability and wrinkle resistance of polyester. Perfect for workwear requiring minimal ironing.
- **Best for:** Weekend wear, casual office Fridays, and outdoor events.

Pro tip: Denim blends with 2-3% elastane provide flexibility—great for active lifestyles without sacrificing style.
